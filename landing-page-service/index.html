GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions.

What is included in the $79 package?

The package includes one landing page, responsive layout, brand-tailored styling, content arrangement, one revision and an estimated delivery time of 3–5 business days.

Do I need to provide my own content?

Yes. Please provide your business information, logo, images and other relevant materials. We can help arrange the supplied content into the page structure.

Does the package include a domain and hosting?

No. Domain registration and paid hosting are not included. Any required paid services will be discussed separately.

Can you build an online store or advanced booking system?

The starter package is for a simple one-page landing page. Checkout systems, custom backends, databases, advanced forms and complex functionality require a separate discussion and quote.

How long will the project take?

Estimated delivery is 3–5 business days after the required content and materials have been received. More complex requirements may need a different timeline.
